# ProGylls — Product Manager Gym for Skills

A **practice-first platform for Product Managers** to continuously build, sharpen, and maintain their PM skills.

A place where PMs can regularly come back to **practice case studies, SQL, analytics, metrics, prioritization, product thinking, frameworks, business, technology, AI, strategy, and more.**

The user should be able to **choose what they want to practice, solve challenges, receive feedback, identify gaps, and track their growth** across different areas of Product Management.

The rest is for the team to figure out:

**What should PMs be able to practice? What should the practice experience look like? How should challenges be structured? How should users be evaluated? What role should AI play? How should progress work? What makes PMs come back? What is the MVP? What should be built?**

*Let the team plan, decide, design, and build it.*
